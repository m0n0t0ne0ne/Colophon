First release of Colophon, a free and private audiobook player for Android.

**Download `colophon.apk`** on your phone, open it, and allow the install if Android asks. It needs 64-bit Android 8.0 or later.

- Plays M4B, M4A, MP4, AAC, MP3, FLAC, Ogg, Opus, and WAV, as single files or folders
- Reads existing chapters, and can find chapters and titles on your phone
- Lets you review and edit chapters without changing your audio files
- Groups series, saves your place in every book, and supports custom covers and a dynamic background

This is an early release, and the analyzer currently understands English only. Please report problems in Issues.
